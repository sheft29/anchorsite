The Philosophy

Most of what makes a life good is already within reach. What helps is something honest and specific to return to, small enough to actually do.

That is where the name comes from. One small, worthwhile action becomes an anchor in your day, a fixed point that holds however the rest of it goes. The mark carries the rest of the idea: the wheel is your agency, since every Anchor is yours to take or pass on, and the star at the top is the compass, the direction you set for yourself.

Your Anchor for today (about five minutes)
Today’s Anchor · Peace · 5 minutes

Ten returns to one sensation.

Sit somewhere quiet and rest your attention on one sensation: your breath at the nostrils, your feet on the ground, or a steady sound. When your attention wanders, notice it and come back. Count ten returns.

Why this

You said mood and stress are where you have the most room to grow, and that quiet time alone is when you feel most like yourself.

Worth knowing

Noticing that your mind has wandered and bringing it back is the exercise itself, so every drift gives you another repetition. Drifting is also the norm: in a study that checked in with more than two thousand people at random moments, their minds were on something other than what they were doing almost half the time (Killingsworth & Gilbert, 2010).

On campus

If you’d like a quiet place for it, the Meditation Room in Richter Library (second floor, Room 215E) is open whenever the library is, with no appointment needed. Phones stay off inside.

About

Anchor began as a TEDxUMiami talk on Gen Z mental health. Its central point was that how life feels depends a great deal on whether what you do each day lines up with what you value, and that the drift between the two usually happens quietly, one ordinary day at a time. Anchor turns that idea into something you can do. Each Anchor is meant to bring what you do a little closer to what you value. The idea is that narrowing that gap quiets the distorted thinking it tends to feed, while building satisfaction and a sense of agency over your own life. For now, I read every response and choose each Anchor by hand.
